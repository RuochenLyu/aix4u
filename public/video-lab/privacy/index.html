Video Lab — Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Video Lab is an internal publishing tool operated by Ruochen Lyu (aix4u). It is used only by our own team to upload and manage videos on YouTube channels that we own and operate. It has no public sign-up, no public user interface, and no third-party users.

YouTube API Services

Video Lab uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with content managed through Video Lab you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

Through Google OAuth, Video Lab accesses only the YouTube channels that we own. It uses:

  • OAuth credentials for our own channels, to authorize API requests;
  • metadata of our own channels and videos, such as titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists and privacy status;
  • YouTube Analytics reports for our own channels;
  • public comments posted on our own videos, so that we can read, reply to and moderate them.

How we use it

We use this information only to publish videos to our channels, keep their metadata accurate, verify upload status, and understand how our own content performs. We do not sell or rent it, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except where required by law.

Storage and retention

Information is stored on systems controlled by our team. OAuth credentials are kept in local configuration that is not published. We keep information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes above and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Revoking access and deletion

Access granted to Video Lab can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. To ask us to delete information relating to you, such as a comment you posted on one of our videos, contact us at the address below.

Cookies

Video Lab is a command-line tool and does not set cookies or use tracking technologies.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
